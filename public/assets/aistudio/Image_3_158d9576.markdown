## Brand & Style

This design system crafts an immersive, high-velocity habit tracking ecosystem tailored for ambitious digital natives, biohackers, and high performers. The atmosphere merges disciplined minimalism with electro-luminescent gaming feedback: deep, distraction-free obsidian voids punctuated by surgical neon glows, energetic progress indicators, and tactile micro-rewards.

The aesthetic fuses **Tactile Dark Glassmorphism** with **Futuristic Performance UI**. Interface elements feel like precision instruments—weighted, crystalline, and alive. Micro-interactions utilize crisp luminous gradients rather than flat tones to trigger momentum, transforming daily micro-habits into satisfying dopamine loops.

## Colors

The palette establishes extreme contrast against pitch-black environments to prevent cognitive fatigue while magnifying state changes.

- **Foundations**: Canvas Background sits at deep void `#0B0E14`, resting Cards at `#151922`, elevated overlays and interactive sheets at `#1D2330`, bordered with precision structural strokes at `#232936`.
- **Primary Kinetic Accent**: Electric Indigo (`#6366F1`) paired with Electric Cyan (`#38BDF8`) for foundational actions, navigation, active trackers, and ambient glow rings.
- **Energy & Momentum**: Neon Amber (`#F59E0B`) and Hyper Flame (`#FF5722`) reserved exclusively for streak counts, critical timers, gamified level milestones, and heatmaps.
- **Completion & State**: Vivid Emerald / Neon Mint (`#10B981`) for habit completions, progress fills, and perfect-day affirmations.
- **Neutrals & Text**: Primary content displays in pure Crisp White (`#F8FAFC`), secondary metadata in Muted Steel (`#94A3B8`), and disabled or passive cues in Dark Slate (`#475569`).

## Typography

Typography prioritizes rapid glanceability, structural clarity, and high data density. **Plus Jakarta Sans** provides geometric rhythm and contemporary authority.

- All quantitative metrics (streaks, countdown timers, completion percentages) must render with `font-feature-settings: "tnum" on, "cv05" on` to enforce monospace tabular alignment and eliminate layout jitter during active countdowns.
- `label-caps` must be applied in full uppercase for category eyebrows, badge tokens, and progress metadata to maximize scannability.
- Maintain high contrast: never drop body text opacity below 60% on dark cards to preserve accessibility in low-light environments.

## Layout & Spacing

The system leverages a mobile-first 4-column fluid layout on phones, transitioning to an 8-column container on tablets and compact split-views. 

- **Outer Margins**: Built around a strict `1rem` (16px) margin on mobile, expanding to `1.5rem` (24px) on wider views.
- **Rhythm**: Driven by an exact 4px/8px modular scale. Habit cards use `space-md` (16px) vertical interior padding with `space-sm` (8px) internal gaps between indicators, check actions, and typography.
- **Safe Zones**: Base sheets reserve a persistent `5rem` bottom clearance zone for floating navigation tabs and dynamic multi-action buttons.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy does not use diffuse gray drop-shadows; it relies on **photonic luminance**, tonal surface stepping, and interior specular strokes.

- **Level 0 (Canvas)**: Solid `#0B0E14`. Zero shadow, flat structural baseline.
- **Level 1 (Default Cards & Tiles)**: Background `#151922` wrapped in a 1px border of `#232936`. Features a 1px top-edge inner highlight `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)` to simulate chamfered glass.
- **Level 2 (Active & Interacted Cards)**: Background `#1D2330` with a 1px border tinted by the card’s accent (e.g., `rgba(99, 102, 241, 0.35)`). Underlaid by a soft chromatic radiance: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(99, 102, 241, 0.18)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals, Overlays & Sticky Drawers)**: High-density glass surface with `rgba(21, 25, 34, 0.85)` background, `backdrop-filter: blur(20px)`, framed by `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)` borders, cast over `0 24px 48px -12px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.8)`.
- **Level 4 (Achievement Glow)**: Dynamic burst elevation for completed routines utilizing dual halos: `0 0 16px rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.4), 0 0 32px rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.2)`.

## Shapes

The interface balances soft ergonomics with sporty precision. Structural interactive items conform to the `rounded-2xl` family (16px / 1rem for standard cards and inputs), providing a modern, tactile feel that fits mobile thumb sweeps.

- **Habit Cards & Dashboard Containers**: 16px radius (`rounded-lg` level token at scale 2).
- **Gamified Streak Chips & Badges**: Fully pill-shaped (`9999px`) to distinguish categorical metadata from primary card structures.
- **Completion Rings & Steppers**: Concentric circular architecture with consistent geometric alignment between SVG strokes and container curves.

## Components

### Habit Action Cards
- **Base**: Grounded on `#151922` with a 1px stroke of `#232936` and 16px corner radius.
- **Active / Focused**: Border shifts to a linear gradient from `#6366F1` to `#38BDF8` with a subtle inner glow.
- **Layout**: Leading interactive habit icon/ring, middle metadata (title, target frequency, time anchor), trailing completion toggle or streak chip.

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA**: Linear gradient fill from `#6366F1` to `#38BDF8`, text in pure `#FFFFFF`, font weight `700`. Elevation produces an ambient cyan-indigo glow (`0 4px 16px rgba(99, 102, 241, 0.3)`).
- **Secondary Action**: Background `#1D2330`, border `1px solid #232936`, text `#F8FAFC`.
- **Destructive / Reset**: Transparent fill with `1px solid rgba(255, 87, 34, 0.3)`, text `#FF5722`.

### Gamified Streak Chips
- Compact pill-shaped badges (`padding: 4px 10px`).
- Flame theme: Background `rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.12)`, border `1px solid rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.3)`, text `#F59E0B`, displaying an animated flame icon and tabular counter.

### Completion Rings & Checkboxes
- Replaces traditional checkboxes with custom 32px circular SVG progress rings.
- Track color: `#232936`.
- Fill transition: Neon Emerald (`#10B981`) stroke animation with dynamic glow on snap completion.
- Internal check icon scales with a spring bounce upon reaching 100%.

### Form Inputs & Time Selectors
- Background `#0E121A`, 1px border `#232936`, 12px corner radius.
- Active focus displays a 1px outer ring in `#38BDF8` with an inset shadow.
- Placeholder text in `#475569`; active input text in `#F8FAFC`.

### Progress Rings & Visualizers
- Multi-habit summary dashboard uses concentric concentric rings (primary ring `#6366F1`, secondary `#38BDF8`, bonus `#10B981`) set against translucent dark glass circular backdrops.
